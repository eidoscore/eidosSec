## 📊 Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Year 1:**
- Marketing spend: $1,200
- Customers acquired: 40
- **CAC: $30/customer**

**Year 2:**
- Marketing spend: $24,400
- Net new customers: 210 (250 - 40)
- **CAC: $116/customer**

**Year 3:**
- Marketing spend: $86,000
- Net new customers: 650 (900 - 250)
- **CAC: $132/customer**

**3-Year Average CAC: $124/customer**

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Assumptions:**
- Average account size: 12 users (blended)
- ARPU (Annual Revenue Per User): $468 ($39 × 12)
- ARPU per account: $4,836 (12 users × $468)
- Average customer lifetime: 3 years (based on 2% monthly churn in Year 3)

**LTV Calculation:**
- LTV = ARPU per account × Average lifetime
- **LTV = $4,836 × 3 years = $14,508**

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**
- LTV:CAC = $14,508 / $124 = **117:1**
- Industry Benchmark: 3:1 is good, 5:1 is excellent
- **Your Ratio: 117:1 is EXCEPTIONAL** (low CAC due to product-led growth)

### Payback Period

**Calculation:**
- Payback Period = CAC / (Monthly ARPU per account × Gross Margin)
- = $124 / ($403/month × 0.9)
- = $124 / $363
- **= 0.34 months (10 days)**

- Industry Benchmark: 12 months is good, 6 months is excellent
- **Your Payback: 10 days is INSANE**
